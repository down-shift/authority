## Question / hypothesis

Does the pilot's rendering sensitivity survive (a) greedy generation with a
strict parser instead of candidate log-probabilities and (b) scale? H1: on the
application task, accuracy differs across the five renderings for ≥27B models.
H2: rendering disagreement > 0.

## Result: negative (Gate-0 recommendation FAIL)

- **None of the three ≥27B models passes.**
  - Qwen3-32B and Gemma-3-27B have a 1.1 pp worst-case gap [0.3, 2.2] and
    1.4% / 2.2% disagreement. The CIs exclude 0, but the effects are far below
    the 5 pp / 5% thresholds. The Holm-adjusted p is 0.096 for both.
  - Llama-3.3-70B W8A8 is perfect on every rendering, with zero disagreement.
- **The 8B bridge model reproduces the pilot effect under generation.**
  - Qwen3-8B has an 8.6 pp gap [5.6, 11.9]: natural_language 91.4% vs
    decision_owner 100%. Disagreement is 13.9%.
  - All 50 flips are **deny→allow**: the model chose the non-owner's proposal.
    None is allow→deny.
- **Interpretation is at ceiling everywhere**: 100% for all models and
  renderings. At 8B, then, the interpretation-correct / application-wrong
  dissociation is present and depends on the rendering (natural_language and
  permission_table). At ≥27B the application errors are nearly gone, so the
  dissociation is too.
- **The direction of errors is consistent.** Every application error at every
  scale is fail-open (deny→allow). The scale is 50 / 4 / 8 / 0 instances for
  the four models.

## Conclusion & implication

On the pilot's toy single-scope worlds (two actors, one field, two
candidates), rendering sensitivity is a **small-model phenomenon**. It's clear
at 8B under both log-prob scoring (pilot) and greedy generation (here). It's
about 1 pp at 27–32B and absent at 70B. By the preregistered rule, Phase 0
**fails**, and `docs/RESEARCH_PLAN.md` §3 names the pivot: format-diverse SFT
for small open agents.

What this does **not** show:
- It doesn't show that large models are invariant on harder authorization
  policies. These worlds are near the ceiling by design.
- It isn't evidence about the Phase-1 benchmark (multi-rule, deny-overrides,
  attribute conditions), the agentic tier, or current models. The current
  models are covered by P0.5, which is reported separately and is not a
  Gate-0 input.
- The only robust signal at scale is the direction of the residual errors:
  always fail-open.
- These are behavioral results only, not a mechanism.

The G0 decision belongs to Jerzy. Options the plan allows:
- (a) FAIL → pivot to SFT-primary;
- (b) waive the gate, on the grounds that the kill test's stimuli were too
  easy to discriminate at scale, and proceed to Phase 1 with harder policies;
- (c) consider the P0.5 addendum first.
